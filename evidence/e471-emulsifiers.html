E471 — Emulsifiers

What BOTIA detected

The label contains E471 or mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids.

What it means on a label

E471 is used as an emulsifier. Its source may be vegetable, animal, or synthetic, depending on the supplier and manufacturing process.

What sources say

Food additive references describe E471 as an emulsifier. The origin is not always visible from the ingredient list alone.

What cannot be concluded

BOTIA cannot confirm whether this ingredient is halal unless the source, supplier information, or certification is clear.

Why BOTIA shows this icon

BOTIA shows this icon because the ingredient may require traceability before a confident halal decision.

BOTIA does not give medical or religious rulings.

BOTIA reads the label and links detected ingredients to documented evidence, traceability criteria, or source information so the user can make an informed choice.
