In our first analysis of real supermarket products, 72% of results were uncertain — not because the ingredients were forbidden, but because the label simply didn't say enough to know.

This isn't a Halal problem, or a sugar problem, or a women's health problem. It's a transparency problem. And it affects everyone.

Transparency first

BOTIA only informs based on the ingredients read from the label. It does not analyse the whole product. It does not certify food. It does not replace medical, religious, legal, or professional advice. It does not see what the label does not declare.
