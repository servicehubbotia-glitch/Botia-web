Woman is not a life stage

A scientific evidence layer for women's biology.

Woman highlights evidence relevant to women's health when that evidence exists — and leaves it out when it does not. It does not mean pregnancy. It does not reduce women's health to reproduction.

Why Woman exists

Between 2020 and 2026, international scientific and regulatory bodies have intensified their focus on how food ingredients — both natural and synthetic — differentially affect women's physiology. Endocrine disruptors, bioaccumulative contaminants, and certain additives have been linked to conditions such as PCOS, endometriosis, early puberty, and breast cancer. BOTIA Woman organises this evidence without alarmism and without hiding uncertainty.

Key areas of evidence

BOTIA Woman connects label ingredients with official scientific evidence in these areas:

  • Endocrine disruptors: BPA, phthalates, parabens — linked to PCOS, endometriosis, and hormonal disruption.
  • Heavy metals: mercury, lead, cadmium, arsenic — bioaccumulative and classified as obesogens.
  • Alcohol and breast cancer: IARC Group 1 carcinogen. No safe threshold identified.
  • Trans fats: industrial TFAs increase cardiovascular risk, especially post-menopause.
  • Artificial sweeteners: emerging evidence on early puberty and hormonal axis disruption.

What BOTIA can and cannot conclude

BOTIA CAN

  • Read declared ingredients from the label.
  • Connect ingredients to official evidence on women's health when that evidence exists.
  • Show uncertainty when the label is silent about origin or processing.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Diagnose or predict any medical condition.
  • Detect undeclared contaminants from packaging or processing.
  • Replace medical, professional, or dietary advice.

Women's health is not a niche. It is half of the population demanding transparent evidence.
