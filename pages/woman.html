Woman

Science has a gender. Food labels don't mention it.

Women's bodies respond differently to certain food ingredients. The evidence exists. It rarely makes it onto the label. BOTIA surfaces it.

Why Woman exists in BOTIA

For decades, nutritional and toxicological research focused on the general population — which in practice meant adult men. Women's specific biological vulnerabilities received less attention. BOTIA's Woman module is built on the evidence that does exist: peer-reviewed studies, EFSA opinions, WHO reports, and IARC classifications that document differential risk for women.

Woman is not a life stage

This module is not about pregnancy. It is not about motherhood. It is a scientific evidence layer that highlights documented risks relevant to women's biology — hormonal sensitivity, higher body fat percentage, endocrine vulnerability, and reproductive system exposure. It applies to all women, at any age.

Two types of signals

BOTIA's Woman module works in two ways. When a specific ingredient with documented risk for women is detected on the label, BOTIA flags it directly. When no specific ingredient is found, BOTIA still surfaces contextual risk information — about packaging, about cumulative endocrine exposure, about fat-soluble compounds — because these risks exist regardless of what the label declares.

Ingredients BOTIA flags for women

These ingredients have documented evidence of differential risk for women's biology:

BPA Phthalates Parabens BHA BHT E171 Alcohol Trans fats

Always-present risk context

These three signals appear regardless of the ingredient list — because the risks they describe are structural, not ingredient-specific:

Endocrine disruptors

Cumulative exposure to substances that interfere with the hormonal system. Women are particularly vulnerable due to higher body fat percentage and hormonal complexity.

Packaging migrants

Chemical compounds that transfer from packaging into food — BPA, phthalates, PFAS. They do not appear on the ingredient list but enter the body through food.

Fat-soluble accumulation

Lipophilic compounds accumulate in fatty tissue over years. They release during rapid fat loss, pregnancy, breastfeeding or menopause — in concentrated form.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect ingredients with documented differential risk for women.
  • Surface contextual risks that do not appear on the label.
  • Connect label ingredients to EFSA, WHO and IARC evidence specific to women.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Diagnose, assess personal risk, or replace medical advice.
  • Detect contaminants or packaging migrants not declared on the label.
  • Tell you what to eat or avoid — that decision is always yours.

The science exists. BOTIA makes it visible.
