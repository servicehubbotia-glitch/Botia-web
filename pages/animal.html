Animal-Origin Ingredient Detected

This product contains an ingredient of animal origin, or possible animal origin.

BOTIA reads the ingredient list and flags ingredients that are confirmed or potentially derived from animals. It does not certify vegan or vegetarian status — it makes the label transparent.

Levels of certainty

Confirmed animal origin

Ingredient is always derived from animals. No ambiguity.

Possible animal origin

Ingredient may come from animal or plant sources. The label does not specify. Only the manufacturer can confirm.

About 'vegan' labels

There is no legal definition of 'vegan' in EU, UK or US law. It is a dietary suitability claim, not a safety guarantee. A product labelled vegan may still contain animal-origin additives if the manufacturer has not verified the full supply chain.

About 'may contain traces of milk/egg'

This warning is for allergy protection — it does not indicate an intentionally added ingredient. Most vegan consumers do not consider cross-contamination warnings as invalidating vegan status.

What BOTIA cannot conclude

  • Whether this product is certified vegan or vegetarian
  • The exact origin of ambiguous ingredients — only the manufacturer can confirm
  • Whether processing aids of animal origin were used but not declared
