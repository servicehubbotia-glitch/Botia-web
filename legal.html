Purpose of this website

BOTIA is an informational service. It reads declared food ingredients and connects them with published scientific, regulatory and transparency sources — WHO, EFSA, FDA, Codex Alimentarius and others. Its purpose is to make label information understandable. It does not sell food, endorse products, or certify anything.

Not medical or nutritional advice

Nothing on this website is medical, nutritional, dietary or professional advice. BOTIA does not diagnose, treat or assess individual health, and cannot determine whether a product is suitable for any particular person. For decisions about your health or diet, consult a qualified professional.

Not a certification body

BOTIA is not a certifying authority of any kind. It does not issue halal certification, organic certification, or any other seal. It reports what a label declares and what it leaves undeclared.
