Trans fatty acids

trans fats, trans fatty acids, partially hydrogenated oil, partially hydrogenated vegetable oil, iTFAs, grasas trans

What it is

Unsaturated industrial fats created by partially hydrogenating vegetable oils to make them solid at room temperature.

Why BOTIA flags it

It flags them because they damage microvasculature, posing a significantly higher cardiovascular risk and hormonal disturbance in women compared to men.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect 'partially hydrogenated' oils or explicit 'trans fat' declarations on labels.
  • Alert consumers of heightened cardiovascular risk and microvascular damage in women.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Measure the exact amount of trans fats in the food if it is below standard regulatory disclosure thresholds.
  • Determine if naturally occurring trans fats from dairy or meat are present versus industrial ones.
