Halal

No prohibited or undeclared ingredients were found on this label.

Halal is not something BOTIA finds. It is what remains when nothing forbidden or unexplained appears.

Why this matters

Under Islamic Law, food must not contain anything unlawful, and must not have been in contact with non-halal substances during preparation. BOTIA reads what the label declares. When no forbidden ingredient appears and no origin is left undeclared, there is nothing on that label to flag.

What BOTIA detected

Nothing. No haram ingredient was declared, and no ingredient was found whose origin the label failed to explain. That absence is the result — not a certification.

BOTIA CAN

  • Read every ingredient declared on the label.
  • Confirm that no clearly haram ingredient appears among them.
  • Confirm that no declared ingredient was left with an undisclosed origin.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Certify Halal — it is not a certification body.
  • Know the slaughter method or the conditions of the facility.
  • See anything the label does not declare.

Nothing was flagged. That is what the label says — and BOTIA reads nothing more than the label.
