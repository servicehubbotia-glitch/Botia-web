Glycerol

E422

E422, glycerol, glycerine, glycerin, glicerina, glicerol

What it is

A clear, viscous polyol liquid used as a humectant and sweetener.

Why BOTIA flags it

Can be produced from either vegetable or animal fats — religious and vegan tracking uncertainties.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect E422 or Glycerol/Glycerine on scanned labels.
  • Activate texture, animal, and Muslim choice layers.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Verify if obtained from animal or vegetable fat sources.
  • Detect 3-MCPD contaminants above the 0.1 mg/kg limit.
