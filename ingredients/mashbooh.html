Why It Matters

Under Islamic Law, food additives derived from unlawful sources are also unlawful (Codex CAC/GL 24-1997). However, food labels worldwide do not require declaring the origin of many ingredients — flavourings, emulsifiers, enzymes, and gelatins may come from animal, plant, or alcohol-based sources without this being visible on the label.

What BOTIA Cannot Conclude

BOTIA cannot confirm or deny halal status. It signals that the origin is undeclared — the decision belongs to you and your religious authority.
