Acesulfame potassium

E950

E950, acesulfame K, acesulfame potassium, acesulfamo K, acesulfamo potásico, Ace-K

What it is

A calorie-free synthetic intense sweetener about 200 times sweeter than sucrose.

Why BOTIA flags it

It flags it because it is an artificial sweetener characteristic of ultra-processed foods (NOVA Group 4).

BOTIA CAN

  • Identify E950 or Acesulfame K on scanned ingredient labels.
  • Trigger both the sweetener and E-number information layers.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Assess whether the acesulfame K has been blended with aspartame to mask its metallic aftertaste.
  • Determine the presence of organic impurities below the standard 20 mg/kg purity limit.
