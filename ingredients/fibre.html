Fibre

What it is

Fibre is carbohydrate that is neither digested nor absorbed in the small intestine. It reaches the large intestine, where some of it ferments. A distinction is often made between soluble fibre — such as that in oats, pulses and apples — and insoluble fibre — such as wheat bran and the skins of many vegetables. Some labels declare them separately, but this is voluntary. What matters is that the regulatory definition is not identical in every market. The European Union defines fibre as certain carbohydrate polymers with three or more monomeric units that are neither digested nor absorbed in the small intestine; for polymers obtained from food raw materials or made synthetically, it also requires a demonstrated beneficial physiological effect. In the United States, since 2016, FDA likewise requires evidence of a beneficial physiological effect for certain isolated or synthetic non-digestible carbohydrates before they can be declared as dietary fibre. That means the same substance can be treated differently depending on the definition and evidence accepted in each market.

Why BOTIA flags it

BOTIA flags fibre because it is the field where the definition varies most between markets, and that also affects the carbohydrate figure: in the European Union fibre is declared separately, in the United States it sits inside the total. And because analytical methods differ. Two laboratories can report different figures for the same product depending on the method applied.

BOTIA CAN

  • Read the declared value and keep its unit, its basis and the market.
  • Show the soluble and insoluble subfields where the label declares them.
  • State which definition of fibre applies in that market and whether it forms part of the carbohydrate total.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Tell you what percentage of your own needs this value represents.
  • Compare figures from markets with different definitions without saying so.
  • Work out how much is soluble and how much insoluble if the label does not declare it.
  • Treat an undeclared value as zero.
