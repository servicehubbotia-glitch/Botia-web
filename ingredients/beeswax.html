Beeswax

E901

E901, INS 901, beeswax, bee wax, white beeswax, yellow beeswax, white wax, yellow wax

What it is

A wax obtained from honeybee combs. Food-grade E901 is available as yellow or white beeswax and can be used for functions including glazing, coating, stabilising and texturising.

Why BOTIA flags it

BOTIA flags E901 because it is bee-derived, is used as a food additive, and can perform glazing and textural functions in foods.

BOTIA CAN

  • Identify E901, INS 901, beeswax and common white or yellow beeswax declarations.
  • Trigger the animal-origin, texture and E-number information layers.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Determine the amount present or the exact technological function used in a specific product from the ingredient list alone.
  • Determine the production or bleaching method, or suitability for an individual ethical or religious dietary rule, from the ingredient name alone.
