Shellac

E904

E904, shellac, bleached shellac, goma laca, laca blanqueada

What it is

A purified and bleached lac resin secreted by the female insect Laccifer lacca.

Why BOTIA flags it

Glazing agent of direct insect origin — non-vegan and subject to religious dietary review.

BOTIA CAN

  • Identify E904 or Shellac on ingredient lists.
  • Alert vegan, vegetarian, and Muslim consumers of its insect origin.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Determine whether the shellac used is wax-free or contains up to 5.5% wax.
  • Confirm if treated with unapproved bleaching agents during purification.
