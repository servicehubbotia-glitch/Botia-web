Butylated hydroxytoluene

E321

E321, BHT, butylated hydroxytoluene, hidroxitolueno butilado, 2,6-di-tert-butyl-p-cresol

What it is

A synthetic antioxidant used to preserve the freshness and extend the shelf-life of food fats and oils.

Why BOTIA flags it

It flags it due to scientific investigations surrounding its potential endocrine-disrupting properties and hormonal mimetics.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect E321 or BHT on the ingredient label.
  • Alert users about its ongoing safety re-evaluations and restrictions in related consumer products.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Determine if the BHT has migrated from the packaging material or was added directly to the food.
  • Calculate the exact dietary intake of BHT from a single serving of the scanned product.
