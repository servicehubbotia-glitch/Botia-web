Maltodextrin

maltodextrin, maltodextrina, maltodextrine

What it is

An ultra-processed carbohydrate powder made from the partial breakdown of starches.

Why BOTIA flags it

BOTIA flags it because it is a rapidly digestible carbohydrate that behaves like sugar once digested, while not being classified as a free sugar under WHO criteria. It frequently appears in products that avoid the word sugar on the label.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect maltodextrin and flag it as a marker of ultra-processing.
  • Flag its presence as a rapidly digestible carbohydrate, distinct from WHO free sugars.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Determine the botanical starch source (e.g., corn, wheat, or tapioca) unless explicitly declared.
  • Ascertain the glycemic index or the molecular chain length of the specific maltodextrin used.
