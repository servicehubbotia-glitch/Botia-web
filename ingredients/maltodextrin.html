Maltodextrin

maltodextrin, maltodextrina, maltodextrine, hydrogenated starch hydrolysate

What it is

An ultra-processed carbohydrate powder made from the partial breakdown of starches.

Why BOTIA flags it

It flags it because it is a rapid-acting sugar substitute and a clear marker of ultra-processing (NOVA Group 4) that affects blood glucose.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect maltodextrin and classify the food as ultra-processed (NOVA 4).
  • Trigger a Sugar layer warning for glycemic management.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Determine the botanical starch source (e.g., corn, wheat, or tapioca) unless explicitly declared.
  • Ascertain the glycemic index or the molecular chain length of the specific maltodextrin used.
