Gelatine is collagen processed so that it dissolves and gels — it is found in gummies, yoghurts, mousses and medicine capsules. It is always of animal origin, and the label almost never says which species. The body digests it like any other protein.

  • Detect gelatine and explicit source terms such as pork, beef, bovine, fish or animal gelatine when they are declared.
  • Explain that gelatine is always animal-derived and show the declared species when the label provides it; when it does not, BOTIA can identify the missing source traceability.
  • Identify the animal species when the ingredient list says only “gelatine”.
  • Determine slaughter method or other source details that the manufacturer has not disclosed.
