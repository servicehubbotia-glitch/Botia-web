What it is

A family of starches chemically modified to change functional properties such as thickening, stability, emulsification or resistance to processing. In the EU, the authorised modified-starch E-numbers are specific entries rather than a single generic 'E1400s' code.

Why BOTIA flags it

BOTIA groups these terms because labels may say modified starch or identify a specific modified-starch additive. The family name does not disclose the exact modification unless the label gives the specific name or code.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect 'modified starch/modified food starch' and the listed specific modified-starch names and E-numbers.
  • Show the texture and E-number layers while preserving the difference between a generic declaration and a specific additive.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Identify the exact chemical modification when the label uses only 'modified starch'.
  • Infer the botanical starch source or concentration unless it is separately declared.
