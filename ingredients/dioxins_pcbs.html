Dioxins and PCBs

dioxins, polychlorinated biphenyls, PCBs, furans, dioxinas, bifenilos policlorados

What it is

Highly toxic persistent organic pollutants that bioaccumulate in fatty tissues.

Why BOTIA flags it

Extremely lipophilic — accumulate in women's breast and adipose tissues for decades.

BOTIA CAN

  • Identify high-risk fat-containing ingredients that are primary dietary sources.
  • Provide safety details on maternal body burdens and transgenerational transfer.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Determine the Toxic Equivalency without laboratory analysis.
  • Confirm if the product exceeds the European Tolerable Weekly Intake.
