Fat-soluble accumulation

Fat-soluble accumulation, lipophilic compound accumulation, bioaccumulation in fatty tissue, fat storage of toxins

What it is

The process by which lipophilic (fat-soluble) compounds accumulate in the body's fatty tissue over years. These compounds release during periods of rapid fat loss, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or menopause.

Why BOTIA flags it

BOTIA surfaces this risk because women naturally have a higher body fat percentage, making them more vulnerable to the accumulation of fat-soluble toxins. These compounds can release in concentrated form during key life stages, potentially affecting the woman and, during breastfeeding, the infant.

BOTIA CAN

  • Provide contextual information on how fat-soluble compounds behave in the female body.
  • Connect this knowledge to specific ingredients flagged on the label when applicable.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Measure the actual burden of fat-soluble compounds in a specific person from the label.
  • Predict when or at what concentration accumulated compounds may release.
