Carrageenan

E407

E407, E407a, carrageenan, processed eucheuma seaweed, Irish moss gelose, eucheuman, furcellaran, carragenano, carraghenane

What it is

A family of sulfated polysaccharides extracted from red seaweeds and used to gel, thicken or stabilise foods; E407a is processed Eucheuma seaweed.

Why BOTIA flags it

BOTIA flags E407 and E407a because the label does not reveal their molecular-weight profile. EFSA left its group ADI temporary while seeking better data on low-molecular-weight material and unresolved intestinal inflammatory effects.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect the presence of E407 or E407a and classify it under the texture layer.
  • Provide warning details about low molecular weight fractions or processing aids.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Verify the exact level of low molecular weight carrageenan contaminants below 50 kDa.
  • Detect trace levels of extraction solvent residues used in its isolation.
