Titanium dioxide

E171

E171, titanium dioxide, dióxido de titanio, titanium white, CI Pigment White 6, anatase, rutile

What it is

An inorganic white mineral pigment historically used to whiten and brighten foods.

Why BOTIA flags it

It flags it because it was banned in the EU in 2022 after the EFSA concluded that genotoxicity cannot be ruled out and that its nanoparticles accumulate in organs, including the ovaries.

BOTIA CAN

  • Identify E171 on scanned ingredient lists and issue a high-risk ban warning.
  • Provide specific warnings regarding nanoparticle bioaccumulation in ovaries.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Identify whether a product purchased outside the EU still utilizes E171 unless indicated on the packaging.
  • Detect the exact proportion of nanoparticle-sized (less than 100 nm) titanium dioxide present in the additive.
