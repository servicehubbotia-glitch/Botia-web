Sorbitol

E420

E420, E420i, E420ii, sorbitol, sorbitol syrup, D-glucitol, D-glucitol syrup, sorbitol, jarabe de sorbitol

What it is

A low-calorie polyol sugar alcohol used as a sweetener and bulking agent.

Why BOTIA flags it

It flags it because it can induce laxative effects at high doses and serves as an industrial sugar replacer in ultra-processed products.

BOTIA CAN

  • Identify E420 and flag the mandatory EU warning 'excessive consumption may produce laxative effects' if present above 10%.
  • Trigger the sweetener layer and notify of its low-glycemic properties.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Verify the exact percentage of sorbitol added if it is below the 10% warning threshold.
  • Determine if the sorbitol was manufactured from corn starch or wheat starch.
