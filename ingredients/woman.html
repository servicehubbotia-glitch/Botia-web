Science has a gender. Food labels don't mention it.

Some evidence matters specifically to pregnancy, fetal development, reproduction or a defined difference between sexes. BOTIA shows that evidence.

Ingredients BOTIA flags for women

These entries include evidence relevant to women for a clear reason: pregnancy, fetal development, a reproductive effect from a specific compound, or an observed difference between sexes.

Always-present risk context

These signals can exist regardless of the ingredient list because the risks they describe are structural, not ingredient-specific.

Endocrine disruptors

Interacting with hormones isn't the same as disrupting them. A substance counts as a disruptor only when that interaction causes harm.

Packaging migrants

Chemical compounds that transfer from packaging into food do not appear on the ingredient list.

Fat-soluble accumulation

Some persistent, fat-soluble contaminants can build up in body fat in both sexes.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect declared ingredients backed by an official assessment relevant to pregnancy, development or reproductive health.
  • Surface contextual risks that do not appear on the label.
  • Connect label ingredients to EFSA, WHO and IARC evidence specific to women.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Diagnose, assess personal risk, or replace medical advice.
  • Detect contaminants or packaging migrants not declared on the label.
  • Tell you what to eat or avoid — that decision is always yours.

The science exists. BOTIA makes it visible.
