Konjac

E425

E425, E425i, E425ii, konjac gum, konjac glucomannan, konjac flour, goma konjac

What it is

A water-soluble, high-molecular-weight glucomannan fibre obtained from the tuber of Amorphophallus konjac; it thickens water and can form firm gels.

Why BOTIA flags it

Konjac absorbs a great deal of water and forms a firm gel that does not dissolve easily in the mouth or lose that consistency at body temperature. In bite-sized jelly mini-cups this has caused fatal choking in children, which is why konjac is prohibited in this type of confectionery in the European Union and restricted in other countries. The risk is physical, not chemical: JECFA did not see a need to set a numerical limit.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect E425, konjac gum, or konjac glucomannan on labels.
  • Trigger texture layer and provide choking safety warning.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Verify the exact viscosity or molecular weight.
  • Verify if washed with ethanol for purification.
