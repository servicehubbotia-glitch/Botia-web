Fruit juice concentrate

fruit juice concentrate, concentrated fruit juice, fruit concentrate, apple juice concentrate, grape juice concentrate, pear juice concentrate

What it is

Fruit juice from which water has been removed, concentrating the natural sugars. Used as a sweetener in processed foods while allowing manufacturers to label products as containing 'fruit' or 'no added sugar'.

Why BOTIA flags it

BOTIA flags fruit juice concentrate because concentrating juice removes water, leaving a dense source of fruit sugars without the intact fruit structure. WHO counts the sugars in fruit juice concentrates as free sugars. On an ingredient list it still appears as fruit juice concentrate rather than sugar, so a product can derive much of its sweetness from it without the word "sugar" appearing among the ingredients.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect fruit juice concentrates on ingredient labels.
  • Flag products using fruit concentrates as primary sweeteners despite 'no added sugar' claims.
  • Categorise them as WHO free sugar (fruit concentrate group).

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Distinguish between different types of fruit concentrate used.
  • Quantify the exact sugar content from the concentrate alone.
  • Tell you the total sugar content — check the nutrition label.
