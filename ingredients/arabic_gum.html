Arabic Gum

E414

E414, gum arabic, Arabic gum, acacia gum, gum acacia, Acacia senegal gum, Acacia seyal gum, goma arábiga, gomme arabique

What it is

A dried plant exudate, mainly from Acacia or Senegalia species, composed largely of complex polysaccharides and used as an emulsifier, stabiliser, carrier and thickener.

Why BOTIA flags it

Arabic gum is a plant gum that dissolves in water with very little thickening, so it can be used where other gums would make the product dense. The body does not significantly digest it. JECFA did not see a need to set a numerical limit, and the label does not say how much it contains.

BOTIA CAN

  • Identify E414, gum arabic, Arabic gum or acacia gum when declared on the label.
  • Trigger the E-number and texture information layers.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Determine the amount used when it is not quantified on the label.
  • Determine the exact Acacia or Senegalia source species or processing grade from the generic ingredient name.
