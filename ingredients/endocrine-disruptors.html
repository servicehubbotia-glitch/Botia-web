Endocrine disruptors

Endocrine disruptors, EDCs, endocrine disrupting chemicals, hormonal disruptors

What it is

Substances that interfere with the hormonal system. They can mimic, block or alter natural hormones, affecting how the body functions.

Why BOTIA flags it

BOTIA surfaces this risk because women are particularly vulnerable due to higher body fat percentage and hormonal complexity. Cumulative exposure to endocrine disruptors has been linked to PCOS, endometriosis, infertility, and hormone-sensitive cancers.

BOTIA CAN

  • Provide contextual information on endocrine disruption risk from food contact materials and environmental exposure.
  • Connect this risk to specific ingredients flagged on the label when applicable.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Measure the level of endocrine disruptors in a specific product from the label alone.
  • Assess your personal cumulative exposure or individual hormonal sensitivity.
