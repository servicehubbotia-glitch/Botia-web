Methylmercury

methylmercury, organic mercury, metilmercurio

What it is

A highly toxic organic form of mercury that bioaccumulates in aquatic food chains.

Why BOTIA flags it

Poses extreme developmental risks to fetuses and slower elimination rate in women.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect fish ingredients known to be major sources of methylmercury exposure.
  • Provide safety advice for pregnant and childbearing-age women.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Quantify the actual concentration in the scanned product.
  • Determine if the fish was wild-caught or farmed.
