Steviol glycosides from Stevia

E960a

E960a, E960c, E960d, steviol glycosides, stevioside, rebaudioside A, glucósidos de esteviol

What it is

High-intensity sweet compounds extracted from the leaves of Stevia rebaudiana.

Why BOTIA flags it

Plant-derived sweetener used in ultra-processed formulations to replace sugar.

BOTIA CAN

  • Detect E960a, E960c, E960d and Stevia-derived synonyms on food labels.
  • Identify the specific steviol glycoside subclass when declared.

BOTIA CANNOT

  • Verify whether unapproved extraction solvents were used during manufacturing.
  • Determine the exact ratio of stevioside to rebaudioside A.
